ACCOLADES

Noah has been recognized across global film festivals. His accolades include an Official Selection for Absorber at LA Shorts International Film Festival, an Oscar-qualifying festival (2026), Best Documentary Short at the Los Angeles Film Awards (2020), Best Commercial at Top Shorts (2020), and Webby Honoree for Branded Sports Content (2021). His work has been featured in Shots, Little Black Book, and Shoot Online. He was Emmy-nominated for Main Title Design and has been honored by the Clios, CAANZ AXIS, and Ciclope.

BIO

Noah Conopask directs the moment a person changes course. An athlete gathers herself for one more run. An engine turns over in the paddock. Someone alone in the desert makes a choice they will have to live with. He builds films around moments like these, where a small human detail carries the weight of a big cinematic idea and the polish never sands away the grit.

Self-taught, he came to directing through photography, design, and motion graphics. He started shooting film as a kid and still thinks about what a single frame can say before anyone moves. His images have the precision of a photograph and the momentum of something that could break loose at any second. He is as much at home in a city at full tilt as on a mountaintop at dawn, and he holds scale and intimacy in the same frame.

Noah has directed work for Nike, ASICS, Under Armour, Red Bull, BMW, Bose, HOKA, Patrón, and Gillette. For Bullish, he imagined Wall Street at dawn as a cyberpunk Western, with cowboys on horseback and a guide whose calm holds the center as the city erupts around him. For ASICS’ Win the Long Run, he found the quiet rhythm of endurance and gave the audience time to feel the empowerment of going the distance.

His personal films push further. The Reason, shot on Super 8 at the Barber Vintage Festival, follows the people who keep racing old motorcycles for the love of it. It won Best Documentary Short at the Los Angeles Film Awards and was a Webby Honoree. ABSORBER, which he wrote, directed, and scored, is a dark desert romance about the pursuit of perfection and what it takes from you. It was an Official Selection at the Oscar-qualifying LA Shorts International Film Festival in 2026.

On every project he gets specific early. What should the audience feel? Whose experience are we inside? Which image will stay with them? He carries those answers through prep, the edit, color, and sound. A clear plan leaves room to play, and some of his best discoveries come from what collaborators bring to an idea.

Bring him the image you can’t shake or the story you’ve been waiting to tell. He’ll find the film inside it.
